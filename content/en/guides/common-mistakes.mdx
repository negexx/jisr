# Common mistakes to avoid

This guide gathers the mistakes that cost a year, and sometimes a visa. Every important point links to the official rule behind it. When no official page answers a question, we say so plainly.

## Do not miss the application windows

An application is prepared before the summer. The dates below come from official pages, and they change from country to country.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.campusfrance.org/fr/candidature-procedure-etudes-en-france" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
In France, for a first year of a Bachelor's degree, the prior admission application (DAP) opens on 1 October 2026 and closes on 15 December 2026. The university answers before 30 April 2027, and the student answers before 31 May 2027. No answer from the student counts as a refusal.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.maroc.campusfrance.org/la-procedure-de-candidature-sur-etudes-en-france-de-campus-france" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
Alongside the DAP, the online <bdi>Campus France</bdi> procedure in Morocco for connected programmes lists an earlier close, 15 November 2026. The two calendars therefore differ by procedure. The page for the procedure that concerns you must be checked for its own deadline.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.enseignementsup-recherche.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2026-10/Notice%20dossier%20blanc%20DAP%202027-2028.pdf" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
The DAP file must arrive no later than 15 December 2026. The French test <bdi>TCF DAP</bdi> must be taken no later than 12 February 2027, and the results must be known before 16 March 2027.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/plan-your-application/deadlines-processing-time/" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
Germany has no single national date. The date that counts is the one set by each university, often 15 July for a winter semester. The file must be complete by that date. <bdi>uni-assist</bdi> advises applying at least eight weeks before the deadline, because the assessment takes four to six weeks.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://vu.nl/en/education/more-about/apply-bachelors-programme" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
In the Netherlands, for 2027-2028, <bdi>VU Amsterdam</bdi> lists 15 January for a programme with a limited number of places, 1 April for students from outside the EU and 1 May for EU students. The date applies to the request on <bdi>Studielink</bdi> and to the completed file sent to the university.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/apply/" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
In Hungary, the <bdi>Stipendium Hungaricum</bdi> scholarship does not accept applications after the deadline. For the 2026-2027 campaign, the date is 15 January 2026 at 14:00 Central European Time. An application goes both to <bdi>Tempus Public Foundation</bdi> and to the sending partner in the home country.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.universitaly.it/en/studenti-stranieri" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
In Italy, there is no single national pre-enrolment date. Each university sets its own cut-off. The national date concerns only the visa, 30 November 2026 for the 2026-2027 year and 31 October 2027 for the 2027-2028 year.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="guidance" source="https://www.studyinnl.org/plan-your-stay/how-to-apply" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
In the Netherlands, the general dates on the national site are 15 January for a programme with a limited number of places and 1 May for the others. For a student who needs a visa, the university often asks for an earlier date. It remains necessary to check the page of the programme you target.
</Fact>

## Choose the right channel

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F36519/1" verified="2026-10-11">
In France, a holder of a French or European bac forms wishes on <bdi>Parcoursup</bdi>. A holder of a foreign diploma, such as the Moroccan bac, goes through a prior admission application (DAP) in the <bdi>Études en France</bdi> procedure.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.enseignementsup-recherche.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2026-10/Dossier%20blanc%202027-2028.pdf" verified="2026-10-11">
The DAP file is sent to the cooperation and cultural action service of the French embassy, and not directly to universities. A file sent directly to a university is not examined.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.uni-assist.de/en/faqs/plan-your-application" verified="2026-10-11">
In Germany, whether a university uses <bdi>uni-assist</bdi> is its own decision. Some require it for foreign applicants, others accept a direct application. It is therefore necessary to check the route asked for by the university you target.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.universitaly.it/en/studenti-stranieri" verified="2026-10-11">
In Italy, visa pre-enrolment happens only on the <bdi>Universitaly</bdi> portal, which is free and official. Paying a third party for pre-enrolment is not the official route.
</Fact>

## Translated and legalised documents

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/assemble-your-documents/translations/" verified="2026-10-11">
For Germany, <bdi>uni-assist</bdi> accepts a translation into German or English made by a person or institution authorised to translate under oath. Non-certified translations from an ordinary agency are not accepted.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/education/international-students/prepare-your-stay/meet-admission-conditions/verification-of-academic-records" verified="2026-10-11">
In the Netherlands, <bdi>Leiden University</bdi> states that it does not accept records certified by a notary, a translator or a municipal office. Where online verification is impossible, the document must be legalised or apostilled in the country that issued it. This requirement depends on each institution.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.enseignementsup-recherche.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2026-10/Notice%20dossier%20blanc%20DAP%202027-2028.pdf" verified="2026-10-11">
In France, a DAP application is not examined if it is late, if any section is not filled in, or if a required document is missing. An incomplete file gets no extra time.
</Fact>

## The right language certificate

<Fact kind="official" source="https://ressources.campusfrance.org/esr/diplomes/fr/fle_fr.pdf" verified="2026-10-11">
For a DAP in France, an applicant who is not exempt proves French with the <bdi>TCF DAP</bdi>, a <bdi>TEF</bdi> with a mark of 14 out of 20, or a <bdi>DELF B2</bdi> or a higher diploma. The minimum level asked for is <bdi>DELF B2</bdi>.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.maroc.campusfrance.org/les-certifications-en-francais-obligatoires-pour-campus-france" verified="2026-10-11">
In Morocco, the French certificate is compulsory. The <bdi>TCF</bdi> must be less than two years old when the file is submitted.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.testdaf.de/de/hochschulen/der-testdaf-und-hochschulen/nachweis-der-deutschkenntnisse-fuer-das-studium" verified="2026-10-11">
For a German-taught degree, the recognised certificates are the <bdi>TestDaF</bdi> with at least level <bdi>TDN 4</bdi> in all four parts, the <bdi>DSH-2</bdi>, the <bdi>DSD II</bdi> or the final exam of the <bdi>Studienkolleg</bdi>. Level <bdi>B2</bdi> is mostly enough to enter the <bdi>Studienkolleg</bdi>.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.ru.nl/en/education/application-and-admission/language-requirements/english-language-requirements/minimum-test-scores-bachelors" verified="2026-10-11">
In the Netherlands, one university gives as benchmarks an <bdi>IELTS Academic</bdi> of 6.0 overall and 6.0 in each part, or a <bdi>TOEFL iBT</bdi> of 80 overall and 20 in each part. Tests taken online or at home and the combined <bdi>My Best</bdi> score are not taken into account. The exact thresholds vary by programme.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://portale.units.it/sites/default/files/2026-05/Circolare%202026-2027%20studenti_internazionali_EN_0.pdf" verified="2026-10-11">
In Italy, a degree taught in Italian asks for Italian at level B2 or higher, and a degree taught in another language asks for at least <bdi>B2</bdi> in the language of instruction. An applicant who has not passed the language assessment cannot go on to the other tests.
</Fact>

## Proving the required funds

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.japon.campusfrance.org/fr/modification-du-montant-des-ressources-exigees-pour-les-demandes-de-visa-etudiant-a-compt-er-du-1er" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
For France, from 1 August 2026, the minimum amount of funds required for a student visa is fixed at 877.50 EUR per month. It was 615 EUR per month before.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/living-in-germany/finances/" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
In Germany, since 1 January 2025, a student must prove that 992 EUR per month is available. This sum is placed in a blocked account.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/sperrkonto-388600" verified="2026-10-11">
The Federal Foreign Office states that the blocked-account amount depends on the purpose of the stay and follows the maximum support rates paid to German students. The blocked account must be opened before the visa application.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://conscasablanca.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Studi-universitari-2026_2027-inglese-1.pdf" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
For Italy, the Italian consulate in Casablanca asks for proof of financial means of at least 10,179.85 EUR per year, lawful and traceable, through an irrevocable bank restriction. A one-off deposit is not enough.
</Fact>

## The visa timeline after admission

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.maroc.campusfrance.org/la-procedure-pre-consulaire-je-suis-accepte-sur-etudes-en-france-et-les-cas-d-exemption" verified="2026-10-11">
In France, the process runs in order. Once the file is finalised, <bdi>Campus France</bdi> sends an authorisation to book an appointment with <bdi>TLS Contact</bdi>. Going to <bdi>TLS</bdi> without this authorisation means the file is refused.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.maroc.campusfrance.org/system/files/medias/documents/2026-07/CAMPUS%20-%20FAQ%20Proce%CC%81dure%20pre%CC%81-consulaire%20et%20consulaire%20MAJ%20JUILLET%202026_V29_06_26-LIGHT2.pdf" verified="2026-10-11">
The pre-consular review takes 15 to 20 days. The official page advises booking an appointment as soon as the authorisation arrives, without waiting.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://france-visas.gouv.fr/la-demarche" verified="2026-10-11">
A visa decision usually takes 15 days, and up to 45 days in some particular cases.
</Fact>

<GuideSteps>
  <li>Check that you have a firm offer, and not only an application still under review.</li>
  <li>Complete the pre-consular file and wait for the authorisation from <bdi>Campus France</bdi>.</li>
  <li>Book an appointment with <bdi>TLS Contact</bdi> as soon as the authorisation arrives.</li>
  <li>Open the blocked account, or prepare the proof of funds, before the appointment.</li>
  <li>Allow for the visa time before booking a ticket.</li>
</GuideSteps>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.universitaly.it/en/studenti-stranieri" verified="2026-10-11" freshness="volatile">
For Italy, even with a validated pre-enrolment, missing the visa cut-off loses the year. For 2026-2027 the date is 30 November 2026, and some universities set an earlier date.
</Fact>

## Being wary of intermediaries

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.maroc.campusfrance.org/les-frais-de-dossier-campus-france" verified="2026-10-11">
<bdi>Campus France</bdi> states that no intermediary or private body is recognised or accredited to help with the procedure. Advertisements that promise admission or a visa cannot keep that promise, because no outside structure has that power.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/plan-your-application/deadlines-processing-time/" verified="2026-10-11">
<bdi>uni-assist</bdi> states that it does not cooperate with commercial agencies, and that payments go only through its official portal. An organisation that presents itself as a partner of <bdi>uni-assist</bdi> should be treated with caution.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.universitaly.it/en/studenti-stranieri" verified="2026-10-11">
In Italy, official pre-enrolment is free on <bdi>Universitaly</bdi>. Paying an agency for pre-enrolment is therefore not necessary.
</Fact>
