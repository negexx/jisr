# The Moroccan bac and access to European universities

This guide describes how the Moroccan baccalaureate is recognised for entry to a Bachelor's degree in France, Germany, the Netherlands, Hungary, Italy and Spain. For each country it says whether entry is direct or needs a preparatory year. It does not replace the official pages.

## What this guide covers

The Moroccan bac opens access to higher education in Morocco. In Europe, recognition depends on the country and sometimes on the institution. Some countries take the bac directly. Others ask for a preparatory year before the Bachelor's. The rules below come from official sources.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.nuffic.nl/en/education-systems/morocco/primary-and-secondary-education" verified="2026-10-11">
The Moroccan school system has six years of primary and six years of secondary education, twelve years before university. The bac gives access to higher education in Morocco.
</Fact>

## Your application in four steps

Whichever country you pick, the process follows the same general order.

## France

**Direct entry.** The Moroccan bac allows direct entry to the first year of a licence. There is no compulsory preparatory year. Applications go through the DAP blanche procedure on the <bdi>Études en France</bdi> platform.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.maroc.campusfrance.org/candidature-en-1ere-annee-de-licence-a-l-universite-et-en-paces" verified="2026-10-11">
Holders of the Moroccan bac submit their choices on the <bdi>Études en France</bdi> platform of their country of residence. The <bdi>Parcoursup</bdi> procedure does not concern them.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.maroc.campusfrance.org/4la-soumission-et-le-paiement-du-dossier-de-candidature" verified="2026-10-11">
DAP blanche covers the first year of a licence at university. It is an admission procedure. It is not a preparatory year.
</Fact>

A French level of <bdi>B2</bdi> is required under DAP. Campaign dates change each year and must be read again on the Moroccan <bdi>Campus France</bdi> page for each intake.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.france-education-international.fr/article/tcf-dap?langue=fr" verified="2026-10-11">
Under DAP, the TCF tout public with the compulsory written expression test is required. DELF B2 or DALF are also taken into account.
</Fact>

## Germany

**Indirect entry.** The Moroccan bac alone does not give direct entry to a Bachelor's. It is the route to the <bdi>Studienkolleg</bdi>, a preparatory year that ends with the <bdi>Feststellungsprüfung</bdi>.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.uni-assist.de/tools/laenderhinweise/laenderdetails/country/ma" verified="2026-10-11">
In Germany, a baccalaureate alone does not give the right to direct studies. It prepares for the <bdi>Studienkolleg</bdi> and the <bdi>Feststellungsprüfung</bdi>.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/requirements/studienkollegs/" verified="2026-10-11">
Without the school-leaving certificate that grants university access, direct entry into the German university system is not possible. Preparation at a <bdi>Studienkolleg</bdi> usually takes two semesters.
</Fact>

A Moroccan licence professionnelle, or one or two years of study at a recognised Moroccan university, can open other routes. This point is to be checked case by case.

## Netherlands

**Direct entry at universities of applied sciences, indirect at research universities.** <bdi>Nuffic</bdi> places the Moroccan bac between <bdi>HAVO</bdi> and <bdi>VWO</bdi>. A research university asks for <bdi>VWO</bdi> or a first year of <bdi>HBO</bdi>. A university of applied sciences normally accepts <bdi>HAVO</bdi>.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.nuffic.nl/en/education-systems/morocco/level-of-diplomas" verified="2026-10-11">
<bdi>Nuffic</bdi> places the Moroccan attestation du baccalauréat at least at <bdi>HAVO</bdi> level. That level stays below <bdi>VWO</bdi>.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/binaries/content/assets/ul2student/study-abroad/incoming-exchange/higher-education-system-in-the-netherlands.pdf" verified="2026-10-11">
For entry to a research-oriented Bachelor's, students need <bdi>VWO</bdi>, or the complete first year, 60 credits, of a Bachelor's at a university of applied sciences.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://hz.nl/en/organisation/education-system" verified="2026-10-11">
In principle, a diploma at <bdi>HAVO</bdi>, <bdi>VWO</bdi> or <bdi>MBO</bdi> level 4 allows enrolment in a higher professional education programme.
</Fact>

The evaluation of diplomas by <bdi>Nuffic</bdi> is advisory. Each institution decides on enrolment.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.nuffic.nl/en/study-and-work-in-the-netherlands/applying-for-a-credential-evaluation" verified="2026-10-11">
A credential evaluation is not legally binding. Universities and universities of applied sciences decide themselves which students they take.
</Fact>

## Hungary

**Direct entry.** There is no national preparatory year before the Bachelor's. Each university decides whether the Moroccan bac is equivalent to the <bdi>érettségi</bdi>. Some programmes add an entrance exam.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://studyinhungary.hu/study-in-hungary/menu/studying-in-hungary/entry-and-admission-requirements.html" verified="2026-10-11">
For a Bachelor's, a secondary school diploma is required, translated into Hungarian or English, with the transcript of courses and grades. Some programmes also ask for an entrance exam.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.uni-corvinus.hu/downloads/baii.1pwskcb/recognition-procedure-shdiaspora.pdf" verified="2026-10-11">
Recognition of the diploma lies with the institution where the applicant wants to study. There is no single central decision.
</Fact>

A Morocco-specific line on the Hungarian country list was not found. This point is to be checked.

## Italy

**Direct entry if the diploma covers twelve years.** The Moroccan system counts twelve years before university, so the integration year does not apply to the Moroccan bac. The Morocco-specific line on the official list is to be checked.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.cimea.it/EN/pagina-riconoscimento-accademico" verified="2026-10-11">
A foreign school-leaving certificate allows entry to first-cycle programmes if it was obtained after an itinerary of at least twelve years of schooling.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.uniroma3.it/int/wp-content/uploads/file_locked/2026/06/GUIDE-HOW-TO-APPLY-ENG-2026-27.pdf" verified="2026-10-11">
For a Bachelor's, at least twelve years of schooling are required. If the school period was shorter, a university studies certificate is required on top.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.universitaly.it/en/first-steps" verified="2026-10-11">
Pre-enrolment is done on the <bdi>Universitaly</bdi> portal. This is the only procedure that provides the document needed for the visa application.
</Fact>

Campaign dates and the documents asked for vary by university. They must be read again on each institution's page.

## Spain

**Indirect entry.** A Moroccan baccalaureate does not give automatic direct access to a <bdi>Grado</bdi>. The route is the <bdi>homologación</bdi> of the bac to the <bdi>Título de Bachiller</bdi>, then the <bdi>UNEDasiss</bdi> credencial and the <bdi>PCE</bdi> competence exams, then the <bdi>nota de corte</bdi> of each programme. Each region and each university decides admission, and there is no single national portal.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/mc/convalidacion-homologacion/convalidacion-no-universitaria/ensenanzas/homologacion-graduado.html" verified="2026-10-13">
The <bdi>homologación</bdi> to the <bdi>Título de Bachiller</bdi> is granted by the Spanish ministry of education. It is the step that places a foreign secondary diploma at the level of the Spanish school-leaving certificate.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://unedasiss.uned.es/home" verified="2026-10-13">
<bdi>UNEDasiss</bdi> evaluates records from systems outside the European Union and runs the <bdi>PCE</bdi> specific-competence exams. The admission mark combines the record and the exams, and each university sets the <bdi>nota de corte</bdi> for each Grado.
</Fact>

The teaching language and the level asked for are read on each programme page. Living costs have no official national figure for 2026, so the programme page links to <bdi>Numbeo</bdi> instead of showing an amount.

## What "to be checked" means

Some points in this guide do not come from an official page read directly. They carry the note "to be checked". These are the following items.

- The Morocco line in the German <bdi>anabin</bdi> database.
- The French campaign dates, which change each intake.
- The Morocco line on the Hungarian country list.
- The Italian confirmation that the Moroccan bac falls under the twelve years.

We prefer to write "to be checked" rather than guess.
