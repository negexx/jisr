# Studying in Europe after the Moroccan bac

This guide explains how a Bachelor's application works in Europe when you sit your bac in Morocco. It does not replace the official pages. It helps you know where to look, what to compare and what still needs checking on each programme page.

## The five destinations covered

Jisr follows five countries for a Bachelor's degree for now.

- **France.** Applications usually go through the <bdi>Études en France</bdi> platform for Moroccan bac holders.
- **Germany.** The Moroccan bac on its own does not lead straight to a Bachelor's. A preparatory year, the <bdi>Studienkolleg</bdi>, often comes first.
- **Netherlands.** Central registration runs on <bdi>Studielink</bdi>, but some institutions use a different route.
- **Hungary.** Applications run through the <bdi>Tempus Public Foundation</bdi> system, often for the <bdi>Stipendium Hungaricum</bdi> scholarship.
- **Italy.** Visa pre-enrolment happens only on the <bdi>Universitaly</bdi> portal.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.uni-assist.de/en/tools/info-country-by-country/details-country/country/ma/" verified="2026-10-11">
In Germany, a Moroccan Baccalauréat on its own does not grant direct entry to a Bachelor's programme. It is the route to the <bdi>Studienkolleg</bdi> and its final exam, the <bdi>Feststellungsprüfung</bdi>.
</Fact>

## The journey in four steps

Whichever country you pick, the process follows the same general order. Each step then depends on the exact programme.

<GuideSteps>
  <li>Describe your profile. Your bac, your languages and what you want to study.</li>
  <li>Compare realistic options. The programmes that seem to fit your profile, with what still needs checking.</li>
  <li>Estimate the cost and look for scholarships. Tuition, living costs and possible support.</li>
  <li>Prepare the application. Documents, deadlines and the visa.</li>
</GuideSteps>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.maroc.campusfrance.org/la-procedure-de-candidature-sur-etudes-en-france-de-campus-france" verified="2026-10-11">
For the 2026-2027 campaign, the <bdi>Campus France</bdi> procedure in Morocco runs from 1 October 2026 to 15 November 2026, 23:59 Moroccan time.
</Fact>

<Fact kind="official" source="https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/requirements/application-process/" verified="2026-10-11">
Assessment of your certificates by <bdi>uni-assist</bdi> costs 75 EUR for your first programme choice and 30 EUR for each additional programme in the same semester.
</Fact>

## How to read a source

Every important fact on Jisr carries a label that says what kind of fact it is.

- **Official.** A requirement published by a university or a public body. The link opens the original page.
- **Estimate.** A range we work out from published figures. The real amount can differ.
- **General guidance.** Help to plan, not a rule.

The label also shows the date of the last check. Information checked a long time ago is flagged.

## What "to be checked" means

When a page says "to be checked", we have not found the answer on an official page, or the source does not publish that point. We say so instead of guessing.

<Fact kind="official" source="https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/apply/" verified="2026-10-11">
For the 2026-2027 campaign, the <bdi>Stipendium Hungaricum</bdi> deadline is 15 January 2026 at 14:00 Central European Time.
</Fact>

## Questions to settle before choosing

- Which teaching language suits you, and which level is asked for.
- Does the country expect a preparatory year before the Bachelor's.
- What is the calendar for the application and the visa.
