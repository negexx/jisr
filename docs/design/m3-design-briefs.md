# M3 design briefs (approved by the product owner, 2026-09-30)

Output of plan task M3-T0. Inputs: `PRODUCT.md`, the M3 plan, ADR 0005. `DESIGN.md` is generated by `/impeccable document` after the first UI is built and supersedes the visual details here. One brief per page; each is pasted into the matching UI task brief (T8 to T12).

## Shared direction (all pages)

- **Register:** product.
- **Colour strategy:** Restrained. Warm-tinted neutrals plus one deep teal-ink accent (OKLCH hue about 205, dark, low chroma), used only for primary actions, the current step and links. Status colours (green, amber, red) are reserved for match status and always come with an icon and a text label; never colour alone. No `#000`/`#fff`.
- **Scene and theme:** an 18-year-old on a mid-range Android phone, at home in the evening or in a school corridor in daylight, on patchy data, deciding alone. Light theme only in M3 (dark is a later token swap).
- **Anchors:** Wise onboarding (progress feel, friendly controls, honest fee tables), gov.uk forms (plain labels, error summary, one question per screen), Campus France (trust and official-source signalling). Warmth lives in shape and rhythm (rounded controls, encouraging progress), never in promotional copy.
- **Type:** Geist (Latin) and Noto Sans Arabic (Arabic), both already loaded. Fixed rem scale at a 1.2 ratio (14 / 16 / 19 / 23 / 28); inputs at least 16px. Line height 1.5 Latin, about 1.75 Arabic; Arabic about 1px larger optically. Prose capped at 65ch. Latin names, URLs, numbers and currency codes inside `<bdi>` in Arabic.
- **Motion:** 150 to 200 ms, only to convey state, none under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No page-load choreography.
- **Bans:** side-stripe borders, gradient text, glassmorphism, identical icon-card grids, hero-metric templates, modal as a first thought, em dashes in copy.
- **Layout:** mobile-first (ADR 0005 item 12) with designed desktop layouts, logical properties only, directional icons `rtl:rotate-180`, tap targets at least 48px on phone.

## Brief 1: Create Profile (approved)

1. **Summary.** A 4-step form plus a review step. A Bac holder enters their profile, saved only in their browser. It must feel short and answerable by someone who does not know the vocabulary, while collecting enough for honest matching.
2. **Primary action.** Finish the current question and press Next; finally "See my matches". One primary button per screen.
3. **Direction.** Shared direction. Page specifics: large round controls (min 48px), a step header with a progress bar that also has a text label ("Step 2 of 4: Languages"). Nothing decorative.
4. **Scope.** Production-ready, interactive, fr/en/ar, phone and desktop.
5. **Layout.** Phone (390): single column, step header on top, question group, sticky bottom bar with Back (secondary) and Next (primary) within thumb reach. Desktop (1280): centred form column about 640px, a quiet aside from 1024px with "Why we ask" for the step and a "Stays on your device" note with Clear my data; on phone the same content is an inline collapsible "Why we ask?" under the step title. Tight spacing inside a field (label, hint, control, error), generous between fields. Steps: 1 Your Bac (nationality, credential country/type/track/status, overall grade "/ 20", subject grades with add-row, years of higher education); 2 Languages (fr and en rows always, CEFR or "I don't know", certificate select filtered to that language); 3 What you want (fields, study languages, destinations (empty = all), public/private); 4 Budget (optional, EUR, note that budget never hides programs); Review (answers with Edit links, "See my matches").
6. **States.** Loading (reference-data skeleton shaped like step 1); data unavailable (plain error with Retry); no credential types published (EmptyState: still verifying official data); restored draft notice; reset notice (invalid stored profile removed, said plainly); storage blocked notice (answers lost when the page closes); edit mode (prefilled, "Save changes"); field errors inline plus an error summary with links (out-of-range grade names the scale); saved then go to Matches.
7. **Interaction.** Next validates the step then moves focus to the new step heading; Back never loses data; autosave draft locally; Enter submits the step; native selects and inputs (mobile pickers, bundle size), Radix only for the clear-data AlertDialog and the reasons Collapsible; numeric fields `inputMode="decimal"`, `dir="ltr"`, accept "14,5" and "14.5"; never a GET form, no values in the URL; RTL mirrors step order, Back/Next icons and progress fill.
8. **Content.** Every field: visible label plus short hint. Plain, calm voice ("Your Bac", "What was your overall grade?"). "I don't know" is a real option that leads to a "to check" result later, not an error. Never "eligible" or "admitted". Subject rows 0 to about 8. Arabic with Latin digits. Official names (Baccalauréat) untranslated with a gloss.
9. **References for implementation.** interaction-design, spatial-design, typography, ux-writing, adapt (phone and RTL).
10. **Open.** Arabic copy is a machine draft until reviewed; the subject list length depends on credential-type data (empty for now).

## Brief 2: My Matches (approved)

1. **Summary.** The results page: programs grouped by honest match status, with cost, deadline and reasons, for a student who has just finished the profile and wants to know what is realistic.
2. **Primary action.** Open a program that looks possible (the card title is the link, with "See details" as a secondary affordance). With no profile: "Create my profile".
3. **Direction.** Shared direction. Flat row-cards with a text-first hierarchy, not an icon-card grid. The status badge is the only tinted element and always has icon plus label.
4. **Scope.** Production-ready, interactive, fr/en/ar, phone and desktop.
5. **Layout.** Phone: profile summary strip (chips: fields, destinations, budget, "Edit profile"), then a one-line counts summary. Groups in order: checkmark "Published requirements appear to be met" and warning "Something to check" open; foundation group "Possible through a foundation year" opens with a bordered, tinted explanation box (what a foundation year is, what to check); cross "Published requirements not met (N)" collapsed with the count visible; "N hidden by your preferences" as a quiet toggle at the end with counts per mismatch reason. Card: program name and institution with city (`<bdi>`), degree and teaching languages, status badge, then a compact definition list (tuition official or "not published, to be checked"; yearly cost range with an "estimate" tag and the 10-month note; next deadline or "Closed, next dates not yet published"; application method), each fact group with a compact SourceBadge (kind, verified date, freshness), then the top 2 or 3 reasons as short lines saying what to check and where, then "See details". Desktop: two columns, cards in about 720px, sticky right column with the profile summary and Edit profile; facts in a 2-column grid inside the card.
6. **States.** No profile (CTA EmptyState); loading (skeleton cards); no programs published ("We are still verifying official data; no programs are published yet"); all hidden by preferences (toggle prominent); load error (plain message with Retry); text notes ("shown in French", "translated automatically"); aging date emphasised, stale "may be outdated, check the official page"; data updated while open ("reload").
7. **Interaction.** Groups are sections with headings. The collapsed cross group and the hidden toggle are native disclosure controls. Card title is the primary link; the whole card is not a click target. Skeleton then content with no layout jump. Visible focus, same logical tab order in RTL.
8. **Content.** Never "eligible", "admitted" or a percentage. Every warning reason says what to check and where. Costs are ranges with the 10-month note. Dates DD/MM/YYYY. 0 to about 30 cards, no pagination in M3.
9. **References.** spatial-design, typography, color-and-contrast, ux-writing, adapt.
10. **Open.** Phone card density (collapse reasons behind "Why?" at 3 or more) is decided in build with a screenshot review.

## Brief 3: University / Program page (approved)

1. **Summary.** One statically generated page per program with every fact, its source and its freshness. A reference page and the place where the student's personal result is explained.
2. **Primary action.** Understand whether the program fits, then start the application through the official link. No save button in M3.
3. **Direction.** Shared direction; the most restrained page, because it is the trust surface. Source and kind labels are quiet but always present; "official", "estimate" and "guidance" have three visually distinct badge treatments so an estimate never looks official.
4. **Scope.** Production-ready, static plus client personal leaves, fr/en/ar, phone and desktop.
5. **Layout.** Header: program name, institution and city, degree, duration, languages. Page banner: aging or stale banner computed on the client from the worst freshness across the page's facts. Desktop: two columns, facts (overview, admission requirements, tuition, cost of living, deadline and how to apply, required documents, scholarships, official links, last verified) beside a sticky personal column (match box, next deadline, cost range with budget fit). Phone: single column, personal box first, then sections, with a collapsed "On this page" jump list under the header. Cost: `CostBreakdown` compact table with 7 fixed rows (tuition, housing, food, transport, insurance, application and visa, setup), each with its own kind badge and range, missing lines "to be checked", total as a range ("partial total" when a line is missing), the 10-month note directly under the table and next to housing and food. Requirements: each rule in general terms with its own SourceBadge; partial coverage shows "We have not published all requirements".
6. **States.** Personal box: no profile (CTA), loading (skeleton), load error (Retry); snapshot updated after the page was built ("Data was updated, reload"); server placeholder makes no freshness or deadline claim before mount; past-only deadlines "Closed, next dates not yet published"; aging = emphasised verified date, stale = page banner plus chip on the fact; unknown slug = 404.
7. **Interaction.** Sticky column on desktop only. Section headings are real headings with anchors; official links use `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, marked as external, `<bdi>` in Arabic. Facts readable without JS. Personal leaves hydrate without layout shift.
8. **Content.** Tuition is an official single fee or "not published, to be checked". Costs are ranges with the 10-month assumption. Documents show "translation needed: yes / no / unknown". Guidance is labelled "general guidance", never official. Last verified DD/MM/YYYY per fact and the oldest at page level. Arabic with Latin digits.
9. **References.** spatial-design, typography, color-and-contrast, ux-writing, responsive design.
10. **Open.** Scholarships are a linked list in M3 (the finder is M4). Sticky column height on short desktop viewports is checked in the browser.
