## Cross-cutting findings

- Most gaps are source or gate failures, not missing facts. Italy: every programme and every university is already in the curated registry (`scripts/import/registries/it-pages-1..4.json`).
- National pages can stand as evidence for a country-wide fact (precedent: Campus France Maroc tuition, French level, calendar). Owner decision 2026-10-09: national lists that are only "normally / often required" (DE uni-assist, IT circular, NL guidance) are stored with `info_kind: guidance`, the national page as source; the Campus France Maroc procedure list stays `official`.
- **Bavaria:** non-EU tuition is being introduced "from summer semester 2027 for newly enrolled students" (FAU: "between 1,000 and 6,000 euros per semester"). About 10 existing Bavarian records have tuition 0 (HAW hrk264, hrk287 x3, Landshut Dingolfing, Aschaffenburg, Augsburg, Deggendorf, Passau) and may be wrong for the next intake: re-check before the next release.
- **France cost lines:** from 1 August 2026 the visa minimum resources are 877.50 EUR per month (was 615): https://www.japon.campusfrance.org/fr/modification-du-montant-des-ressources-exigees-pour-les-demandes-de-visa-etudiant-a-compter-du-1er. Crous offers a 1 EUR meal to all students since 4 May 2026 (https://www.lescrous.fr/2026/04/le-repas-a-1-euro-pour-tous-les-etudiants-deploye-a-partir-du-4-mai-2026/). Check the `fr-official` food line (300/month) and any visa wording against both.
- Host allowlist (`isOfficialHost`, `scripts/import/scholarships.ts`) accepts ind.nl, rijksoverheid.nl, esteri.it, campusfrance.org, daad.de, service-public.fr, *.gouv.fr; it does NOT accept diplo.de, auswaertiges-amt.de, bundesregierung.de, stipendiumhungaricum.hu, studyinhungary.hu, bkk.hu, lescrous.fr, university or Studierendenwerk sites (relevant for cost and scholarship tasks).
- The Study Finder (Tempus Public Foundation) written permission is still pending; any new Study Finder run needs it flagged to the owner.
- A table PDF whose numbers have no currency sign next to them fails the "currency next to number" rule; image-only PDFs cannot be read (no OCR).
