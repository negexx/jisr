# Route funding guidance (CROUS, BAföG): as built

Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-route-funding-guidance.md` (kept on the `feat/italy` branch, where it was written). Built 2026-10-05 on `feat/route-funding-guidance`; waiting for the owner to approve the three-language wording of the two notes.

- `guidance` lives on the admission route: items `{ id, source, info_kind: "guidance" (a literal), note: LocalizedText }`, provenance-checked like route conditions, never evaluated and never read by `src/domain` (tests show the match is identical for all four outcomes, also with stale guidance).
- The static programme page reads the routes of every origin into the programme's country (`routeGuidanceFor`, `src/lib/snapshot/server.ts`; the `text.<locale>.routes.<origin>` shards are loaded too). The field is emitted in the snapshot only when non-empty, so every other route shard keeps its bytes; the format version stays 1 (readers ignore an unknown field: `checkShardShape` only checks the shard's identity).
- The block sits under the scholarships list of the "Bourses" section: an h3 "À savoir sur les aides du pays" inside the section's h2, one line saying these are general notes and not admission conditions, then each note with its SourceBadge ("Conseil général", never official). The origin country is named only when notes come from several origins.
- The page's freshness banner counts the guidance facts (section scholarships, label `admission-routes/<slug>#guidance.<id>`).
- `sourceHostname` decodes punycode (`src/lib/punycode.ts`): the BAföG source shows `www.bafög.de`, not `www.xn--bafg-7qa.de` (the ASCII alias bafoeg.de does not resolve).
- Wording, after the Opus review: the Crous note names who the 2-year rule applies to (not a French, EU/EEA or Swiss national; refugee and protection statuses excepted); the BAföG note says the source does not mention a study visa (the page lists examples, not a closed list) and keeps the hedge "generally". Sources re-read 2026-10-05: etudiant.gouv.fr `bourses-sur-criteres-sociaux-pour-qui-2980` and bafög.de `bafoeg-auch-ohne-deutschen-pass.html`.
- Known limit: the rules are destination-level but stored per route. A second origin would repeat the note under its own label, and an origin without guidance would show another origin's note without one. Fine for Moroccan students only; when origins are added, move the guidance to the destination country.
- JS ratchet: unchanged (`pnpm test:e2e`: program page 183.6 KB initial, 238.1 KB until idle, as before; the block is server-rendered). Browser check done in fr and ar, phone and desktop, on real data (no horizontal overflow).
