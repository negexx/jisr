### Scope: the 31 in-scope programmes

`readDaadProgrammes` targets German programmes whose programme page is a DAAD register page (`www2.daad.de/.../international-programmes/en/detail/<id>`) **and** that hold no intake yet. 39 DE programmes lack intakes; 31 of them point at a DAAD page (the other 8 do not, so they are out of scope for this tool).

### Root cause: the DAAD register page no longer prints the structured audience block

The tool parses this fixed shape (README, "German deadlines from the DAAD register"):

```
Application periods
The following is valid for applicants from: all countries
For the winter semester: until 15 July
```

For the 31 target programmes, that block is **absent** on 25 pages (and on 2 more it is present but already past, 3 are summer-only, 1 is EU-only). The pages now print a free-text application-period field instead, which the tool deliberately refuses to parse ("free text … is not parsed and stays unknown"). ### Cross-check: not a cache problem

The worktree had no DAAD cache, so the dry run fetched the pages live and wrote 31 files to the worktree `.cache/import` (2.0 MB). The 737 MB cache in the **main checkout** (read-only) holds the same 31 URLs, cached **2026-10-02** (at import time); there too only 6 pages carry the audience block, 25 are free text, and the 6-block split matches the live fetch exactly. The page shape is unchanged since import, so the audience-block grammar simply does not match the majority of DAAD pages — this is not a stale-cache artefact and not an environment gap.

### Blocker and recommendation

**Blocker:** the DE intake target (~90 %) is **not reachable with `data:daad-deadlines` as it stands**. DAAD's register pages carry application periods as free text without an audience block on 25/31 in-scope pages, and ADR 0009's rule (a deadline needs a quote that names international/non-EU applicants) is what the tool enforces. Reaching the target would need either (a) a *new* source of international deadlines (the university's own page, via the reader/checker flow), or (b) an owner decision to widen the parser to the new free-text field — which would be a trust-model change and a code change, out of scope here and **not** done. Recommended next step: an owner decision on whether to accept the free-text DAAD field (and how to prove the audience), or fold these 31 programmes into a page-reading batch.

### `ma-bac-to-de` — nothing structured

Quotes that bear on a Moroccan Bac holder:

- uni-assist (Morocco page): **"A Baccalauréat alone does not allow you to study directly in Germany. It qualifies you for the Studienkolleg (preparatory course) and the "Feststellungsprüfung" (university qualification exam)."** — this *is* the route outcome (`foundation`); it is not a new condition.
- DAAD country report (Marokko 2021, PDF): **"Das marokkanische Sekundarschulzeugnis (baccalauréat) wird in Deutschland als Hochschulzulassung nicht direkt anerkannt. Schulabgängerinnen und -abgänger müssen in Deutschland ein Studienkolleg besuchen oder können nach einem Jahr Studium an einer anerkannten marokkanischen Universität direkt zu einem fachverwandten Studium in Deutschland zugelassen werden. Neben einer anerkannten Hochschulzugangsberechtigung ist ein gültiger Sprachnachweis erforderlich."**

Why no structured condition follows:

1. **`prior_study_years_min: 1` is not a route requirement.** The DAAD sentence makes one successful year an *alternative* path to **direct** admission ("oder … direkt … zugelassen werden"), not a condition the base (foundation) route requires. Encoding it as a condition on a `foundation` route would misstate the route.
2. **Conditions on a `foundation` route are never evaluated.** `src/domain/match.ts:82` short-circuits: `if (route && (route.outcome === "foundation" || route.outcome === "not_eligible")) return result([first]);`. Any structured condition added here would be **inert** data (dead), and the reviewer would (rightly) reject it as misleading. Outcome must not change, so a structured condition cannot be made meaningful on this route.
3. **`language_proficiency` cannot be encoded without inventing a language/level.** The DAAD report says "ein gültiger Sprachnachweis" (a valid language certificate) but names neither the language nor a CEFR level. Our German data is largely **English-taught**, so the certificate may be English or German — genuinely ambiguous. The rule schema requires a concrete `language`; choosing one would be an invention.
4. `credential_type_in`/`credential_track_in` with `[ma-bac]` would be tautological with the route's own `credentialType: ma-bac` and add no information.

→ **No change.** Keep the existing `manual_check` (`prior-university-year`), which correctly documents the one-year alternative as advice.

### `ma-bac-to-nl` — nothing structured

Quotes that bear on a Moroccan Bac holder:

- Nuffic, "Level of diplomas": **"Attestation du Baccalauréat — at least a HAVO diploma"** — an explicit *diploma comparison*, not an admission decision (the route note already says so).
- Nuffic, "Higher education":
  - HBO bachelor: **"Admission requirements: HAVO diploma or MBO diploma level 4, sometimes extra requirements, e.g. for subject combinations and/or skills."**
  - WO bachelor: **"Admission requirements: a VWO diploma or an HBO first-year certificate (propedeuse) and sometimes extra requirements (e.g. a specific subject combination)."**

Why no structured condition follows:

1. Neither page states a concrete, checkable requirement **for a Moroccan Bac holder** beyond the diploma comparison. There is no grade threshold (`overall_grade_min`/`subject_grade_min`), no required school subject (`subject_taken`), no prior-study-years rule (`prior_study_years_min`), and no language level (`language_proficiency`).
2. The rule schema has no "credential is comparable to X" rule; the comparison is exactly what the `manual_check` note records.
3. The requirements listed are for **Dutch** diplomas (HAVO/VWO/MBO/propedeuse), which a Moroccan applicant does not hold; they do not translate into a rule over the applicant's credential.

→ **No change.** Keep the existing `manual_check` (`hbo-or-wo-level`).

This wave does **not** move P0-1 ("no programme can reach ✅"): the routes remain `manual_check`-only and programmes remain `requirements.coverage: partial`. The blockers are source-level (the cited official pages do not publish the concrete conditional facts a structured rule needs), so reaching ✅ needs richer sources (per-programme requirement pages via the reader/checker flow) or an owner decision on scope — not a route-YAML edit.
