## Translation rules honoured

Modern Standard Arabic, plain wording for a school-leaver, no Darija; Latin digits; official names kept where the Latin-letter guard allows (`TOLC-I EN`, `IELTS`/`TOEFL`, `HAVO`/`VWO`/`MBO`, `VPD`, `BSA`, `HZB`, `CSA`, `CIMEA`, `SEMAPHOR`, `BIG`, `EC`, `UE`/`EUR`, `CV`, `NC`/`GPA`, `CLIQ`, `NT2`, `NLQF`, `HIV`/`AIDS`, `CAE`/`CPE`/`FCE`); all `fr` digit runs, ALL-CAPS acronyms (2+ letters) and URLs reproduced unchanged; identical `{`/`}` counts; ≤400 characters (longest draft 292); no leading/trailing whitespace. Where a mostly-Latin official phrase would exceed the 40% Latin-letter error threshold, the descriptive wording is Arabic and only the distinctive proper noun is kept in Latin (e.g. `Radboud`, `Konstanz`, `Saxion`).
