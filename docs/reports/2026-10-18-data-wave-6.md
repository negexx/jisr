## 3. Pages: the Spanish search tooling does not work for Spain (gap)

`pnpm data:pages --slugs … --search --topics --topic-list fees,language,deadline,documents --max-queries 60` ran (33 Serper queries) but produced **wrong pages**: for UGR it returned random `revistaseug.ugr.es` article PDFs and a doctoral-contract news post; for UPM, Master's/doctorate pages and unrelated PDFs. Cause: the search **queries** are Spanish (`topics.ts` `WORDS.ES`, `discover.ts` `WORDS.ES`, language `es`) but the **result filters** have no Spanish words — `topics.ts` `PATTERN` (lines 52-58) and `discover.ts` `KEYWORDS` (lines 77-81) know only English/French/German/Italian/Dutch, so Spanish pages score 0 and only accidental cognates ("application", "admission", "document") pass the ≥0.4 cut.

Per the brief's fallback, a curated pages file (`data-staging/pilot/es/pages-curated.json`) was built by fetching the programme's own page (its `officialUrl`) plus the university's own admission/fees/international pages — all fetched and title-checked:

## 5. Two independent blockers for `language_proficiency`

1. **`LANGUAGE_WORDS.es` lacks the Spanish word for Spanish.** `scripts/import/evidence.ts:237` is `/spanish|espagnol|spanisch|spaans/i`. A Spanish page saying "español" / "castellano" can never satisfy `checkLanguages(["es"], quote)`, so neither `teachingLanguages: [es]` nor a `language_proficiency` rule with `language: es` verifies. (Italian has a dedicated `LANGUAGE_WORDS.it` that matches "in italiano"/"lingua: italiana"; Spanish has no such entry.)
2. **The enrichment flow ignores requirements.** `data:evidence verify --enrich` prints *"requirements ignored in enrich mode"* and only writes `FACT_FIELDS`; it "never touches requirements" (README). So **no** requirement rule — `language_proficiency` or `manual_check` — can be added to an already-promoted record through the prescribed enrich flow, even if the verifier accepted it. The UCM B2 rule was also rejected by the verifier before this point.

Nothing was forced: the level (B2) was not invented, and no rule was staged.

## 6. Scholarship — no eligible ES scheme; proposal refused

Researched national and regional schemes (2026-08/09 convocatorias). **None is open to a Moroccan Baccalauréat holder applying from Morocco:**

- **Beca general (Ministerio, `becaseducacion.gob.es`)** — requires Spanish/EU nationality, or for non-EU a **residence permit**; "no es suficiente tener concedida la autorización de estancia". Not open to entrants from abroad.
- **Comunidad de Madrid** (Becas de Excelencia, Fondo Social, alojamiento) — "Poseer nacionalidad española o la de otro país de la UE. Las personas extranjeras no comunitarias deberán acreditar la condición de residente en España". Same for the UAM/UC3M programs funded by the CAM.
- **Fundación Carolina** and **MAEC-AECID** — postgraduate / institutional only (no Grado).
- **UGR "Becas Convenio Marruecos"** (the only Morocco-specific, Grado, at one of our institutions: 14 becas 2026/27) — **restricted to Moroccan students who studied at Spanish schools in Morocco** ("centros españoles en Marruecos"); it does **not** apply to a Moroccan-system Bac holder. It was authored as a proposal and run through `data:scholarship verify` as a live check; it was refused:

```
REJECTED eligibility: no accepted quote says the scholarship is open to international (non-EU) applicants
REJECTED eligibleNationalities: no accepted eligibility quote names MA (the country or its region)
REJECTED level: no accepted quote says the scholarship itself is for bachelor (undergraduate) students
```

The first two refusals are themselves Spanish-wording gaps: `mentionsInternationalApplicants` (§7) has no Spanish, and `NATIONALITY_NAMES.MA` (`scholarships.ts:204` = `morocc|maroc|marokk|north africa`) does not match Spanish **"Marruecos"/"marroquí"**. So even a genuinely eligible Spanish-language scheme could not be verified today. Also, `.gob.es` is not in `isOfficialHost`'s `GOVERNMENT_SUFFIXES`, so Ministry (`…educacionfpydeportes.gob.es`) quotes are not accepted as official either.

Proposal: `data-staging/proposals-scholarship/es-ugr-convenio-marruecos.json` (git-ignored, nothing staged). This matches the Spain design spec, which already concluded "No ES scholarship record".
