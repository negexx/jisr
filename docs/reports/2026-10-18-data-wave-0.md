## 2. Duplicate records (P1-2)

### 2a. Eötvös József College — `sf215` / `sf216` (HU)

**Decision: keep `hu-eotvos-jozsef-college-baja-business-administration-sf215`; the removed `...-sf216` slug goes into its `formerSlugs`. The `sf216` file is deleted.**

**Evidence — the two Study Finder pages are the same programme.** Both `https://tempus.tpf.hu/studyfinder/index/show/215` and `.../show/216` render identically:

> Course / programme name: **Business Administration and Management**
> Institution: **Eötvös József College**
> Faculty: **Institute of Business Sciences**
> Branch of Science: Economic Science · Level of studies: Bachelor · Language of instruction: English · Location/city: **Baja**
> Contact website: **https://ejf.hu/en/business-and-management-ba**

The only difference is the (unused) duration line: page 215 says *"Duration of the course / programme: **7 semsters** semesters"*, page 216 says *"**1 semster** semesters"*. Neither figure is stored in the records (both `durationYears: null`). The two records share the same identity and sources, but they are **not** identical in every fact: `sf216` carried a distinct `req-2` note (passport valid ≥ 5 years; documents may be submitted as clear scanned copies) and differently-worded document texts; `sf215`'s document texts are the richer set, and the one unique official sentence from `sf216` was carried over into `sf215` as `req-3` before deleting the file. `sf215` is kept because it carries the lower Study Finder id and its page states a plausible bachelor duration, while `sf216`'s page states an obvious error ("1 semester").

**Schema/alias check.** `formerSlugs` is accepted on program records (`ProgramRecordSchema`, `src/data-schema/records/program.ts:79`) and `data:check` enforces that a former slug is disjoint from every live slug and other former slug (`checkAliases`) — green after the edit.

### 2b. Università di Siena — biotech `l2` / `l8` (IT)

**Decision: keep `it-siena-siena-biotech-engineering-for-health-l8` (field `engineering`, the richer record); the removed `...-l2` slug goes into its `formerSlugs`. The `l2` file is deleted.**

**Evidence — one interclass degree, not two programmes.** The programme's official page `https://biotech-engineering.unisi.it/en/course/program-overview` states:

> "**Delivered entirely in English**, the program stands out for its innovative and international perspective, designed to develop advanced scientific and technological skills essential for addressing the challenges of a rapidly evolving sector. **As an Italian interclass degree, the program is affiliated with the L2 – Biotechnology and L8 – Information Engineering degree classes, allowing students to choose their degree class by the third year of study.**"

This is one course in two degree classes; the Italy import rule is "a course listed under two classes is one programme" (`data/README.md`). The `l8` record's `field: engineering` matches its degree class (L-8 → engineering) and is in Release 1 scope; the `l2` record's `field: allied-health` and its near-empty body (all facts null) were the weaker duplicate. The kept record carries the tuition, intakes, documents and requirement rules, so keeping it loses nothing.

## 3. Stale / redirected sources (P1-3)

### 3a. Bocconi — `international-economics-and-management` → **FLAGGED, left unchanged**

**Evidence.** `curl` on `https://www.unibocconi.it/en/programs/bachelor-science/international-economics-and-management` returns **HTTP 301 → `https://www.unibocconi.it/en/programs/bachelor-science/management`**; the final page is titled `Management | Bocconi University` and its program menu lists "Management" (no "International Economics and Management"). However, the record's application source (`page-2`, `https://apply.unibocconi.it/en_US/courses/course/44-biem-bsc-international-economics-and-management`) is **still live** and titled:

> `[BIEM] BSc in International Economics and Management - Bocconi University` … **Awards BIEM** · **Course code TR08R/GEN/2025** · **Study language English** · **Nominal duration 3 years (180 ECTS)**

and its "More information" link points back at the *redirecting* URL. So the underlying programme "International Economics and Management" (BIEM) still exists on the application portal under its own 2025 course code, while Bocconi's marketing site folded its URL into a page now titled "Management" (a different degree). Renaming the record to "Management" would change the programme's identity on a redirect that may be a site restructure, and would strand the slug's name.

**Action:** left the record as is (name, `officialUrl`, sources unchanged) and flag it for the owner: decide whether the record is the BIEM programme (keep, and find BIEM's current own page) or has been superseded by "Management" (rename + re-slug + re-source). Not resolved in this wave because the evidence is genuinely ambiguous.

**Evidence.** The programme page states:

and the linked "Opleidingsgegevens" page confirms the identity against the RIO code in the record's slug:

(The record is `field: allied-health`, outside Release 1's declared CS/Engineering/Business scope; this wave only repairs the dead source.)
