## Headline

The pipeline ran end to end and added **40 verified facts to 30 programmes**. The wave **did not reach its fact targets**: the strict Netherlands fee rule (institution-wide non-EU `instellingscollegegeld` + study-year gate), the ADR 0009 international-applicant deadline rule, and the second-model checker rejected most candidate facts, and the NL programme pages rarely state a non-EU fee. The shortfall is documented below with the rejection reasons; nothing was invented and every gap stayed `null`.

## Why the targets were not reached (trust-preserving rejections)

Read-time rejections (279 fact/rule rejections; 205 are facts, the rest requirement rules that enrichment ignores). Top reasons:

| Rejected fact | Count | Dominant reason |
|---|---:|---|
| intakes | 55 | 40 × "no accepted quote says the deadline applies to international or non-EU" (ADR 0009 rule) |
| applicationChannel | 47 | 7 × "the quote does not name the channel `nl-studielink`" |
| tuition | 45 | 12 × "the amount does not appear in the quote" (plus study-year gate / institution-wide rule failures) |
| documents | 27 | quote/`quotedAs` not found verbatim, or not tied to a non-EU applicant |
| durationYears | 26 | "quote does not state 4 years, 8 semesters or 240 ECTS" |
| teachingLanguages | 5 | "quote does not mention the language" |

The second-model checker (`qwen3.7-plus`) and `verifyProposal` together removed a large share of the reader's raw candidates: raw→final accepted documents 30→14, intakes 53→21, tuition 70→46, language 79→75. This is the intended trust behaviour (a fact is kept only when a re-fetched quote states it for a non-EU applicant), not a pipeline defect. The reader's own notes confirm the pattern, e.g. "the programme page does not state a fee for non-EU students; the institution-wide page gives … which applies to students who do not meet the statutory fee conditions" (accepted), and "the institution-wide fee pages do not state one non-EU instellingscollegegeld amount for bachelor/voltijd in 2026-2027 or later" (left null).

## Assumptions and notes

1. The expired project key was replaced by the machine's valid `opencode-go` key for the paid read only; no secret was written to any tracked file or printed.
2. `--check qwen3.7-plus` was used (the established convention for NL waves), so the read is a two-model, trust-preserving pass; removing the checker would raise the counts but is not the project's trust model.
3. The brief's stated targets (tuition ≥75%, language ≥85%, documents ≥60%, intakes ≥60%) are **not met** by the specified pipeline on this data. Reaching them needs a different evidence source (e.g. accepted institution-wide fee tables tied to the current study year, and deadline pages that explicitly name non-EU applicants) or an owner decision to relax a gate — this wave did not do either.
4. Only `docs/reports/2026-10-18-data-wave-4.md` is committed; `data-staging/` is gitignored and stays local for the owner.

## Post-review corrections (2026-10-18)

Two promoted records were re-checked after the reviewer flagged them; both were fixed on the branch before the PR.

1. **Capabel Amsterdam — wrong variant corrected.** `nl-capabel-hogeschool-30hc-amsterdam-opleiding-tot-verpleegku-1001o4025` had its `officialUrl` (and its `programme` source) on Capabel's 2-year *Verkorte HBO Bachelor Verpleegkundige (2 jaar)* page, while the W4 facts (duration, language, intakes, documents) were read from the 4-year *HBO Bachelor Verpleegkundige* page — a mix of two variants. The DUO RIO register is decisive: programme `1001O4025` (CROHO 34560, "Opleiding tot Verpleegkundige", international name "Nursing") carries **`STUDIELAST` 240 ECTS** at every current Capabel campus (Utrecht, Amsterdam, Rotterdam, 's-Gravenhage), i.e. the **4-year** full bachelor — the 2-year shortened track is the same CROHO delivered with exemptions, not a separate RIO programme. The sibling campuses (Utrecht, Rotterdam) already cite the 4-year page. Fix applied:
   - `officialUrl` → `https://www.capabel.nl/opleidingen/zorg-welzijn/hbo-bachelor-verpleegkundige`; the single `programme` source repointed there (`last_verified_at` 2026-10-18) and the now-redundant `page-1` removed.
   - `durationYears: 4` kept — quote "De studieduur is 4 jaar.".
   - `teachingLanguages: [nl]` kept — quote "Please note that all our programmes are completely in Dutch.".
   - `intakes` re-read from the served page: Amsterdam **2026-12-08, 2027-04-06, 2027-08-24** (the committed 2026-11-24 / 2027-03-15 were the page's cohort start dates at read time and are no longer served; the page was updated during the day).
   - The verkorte-specific requirement rules (Nuffic NLQF 4 / 3F, and "the Verkort Verpleegkunde bridging programme is part of the admission procedure") replaced with the 4-year page's own rules: language `nl` B2; a HAVO/VWO/MBO 4 diploma or the 21+ admission test; a foreign-diploma evaluation (diplomawaardering).
   - `tuition` stays `null` (the page's "Lesgeld" € 6.816 / € 6.134 is not identified as the non-EU fee).
2. **hs-leiden teaching language — citation generality.** `nl-hogeschool-leiden-21ri-opleiding-tot-verpleegku-1001o4006` keeps `teachingLanguages: [nl]`, sourced (as promoted) to the international-students page: "De meeste opleidingen van Hogeschool Leiden zijn Nederlandstalig." No served page for this record (the programme page, the fees page, or the international page) states a per-programme language of instruction, so the general sentence is the best available evidence and the value is kept as-is.
