## 7. Blockers — why the targets are not reachable with these sources/rules

Both gaps are structural, not tooling faults:

1. **Deadlines are split from the audience.** Italian universities state a selection window and, elsewhere on the
   page, who it is for ("non-EU students residing abroad"), but rarely in the *same* quote. ADR 0009 / the reader
   rule require **one accepted quote that names international/non-EU applicants and carries the date**. 8 of the
   18 rejected intakes were rejected exactly for this ("no accepted quote says the deadline applies to
   international or non-EU applicants"), and 8 more because the model's date was not in the quote.
   Example — `it-udine-udine-management-business-analytics-l18`: the model quoted the extra-EU notice page
   (`avviso-extra-ue-26-27`) with *"DEGREES TAUGHT ENTIRELY IN ENGLISH … period 01/02/2026 al 31/05/2026"*, but
   the quote text itself never says extra-UE/non-EU, so code rejected it (the audience word lives in the page
   title, not the quote). Italy has **no national admission calendar** (only visa deadlines, §6), so there is no
   country-level fallback source.
2. **Non-EU tuition is income/country/ISEE-based.** 9 of 16 rejected tuition facts were rejected by the checker as
   "unclear … not specific to this programme / for Morocco" — the universities publish ISEE bands or country
   categories, not one official non-EU fee for a Moroccan applicant. `verifyProposal` keeps only an amount its
   quote states with a currency; a range needs one quote stating min and max (ADR 0010). Where the page states a
   general "maximum contribution" or a first-year-only figure, it is correctly dropped.

**A broad search augmentation did not help.** A full `data:pages --search --topics --topic-list deadline,fees` pass
over all 65 (184 queries) followed by a second read produced **fewer** accepted intakes (15 vs 16) and tuition
(6 vs 7) than the registry-first read; that run ($0.6662) was discarded. The registry route stays the better one,
and the blockers above are about the rule + the sources, not page availability.

**What would move the numbers** (owner decisions, out of scope here):
- Accept the universities' separated audience+date as evidence (a trust-model / code change), or source each
  programme's own international-admissions page that states both together.
- Accept an income/ISEE-band-based tuition as an `estimate` (currently tuition must be `official`); or find pages
  that state a single non-EU fee for Morocco.
- Decide the field scope of the 10 allied-health/biology programmes (audit §2, P2) — they inflate the denominator.
