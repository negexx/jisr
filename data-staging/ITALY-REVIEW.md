- **Cassino, 3 programmes (600 EUR/year):** the regulation PDF puts Morocco in the group A list only by table layout; the quotes do not show it. Open the 2026/27 regulation (DR 482/2026, art. 3.3) and check that Morocco is in the 600 EUR group.
- **Pavia Artificial Intelligence (390):** the figure includes the 146 first instalment (regional tax + stamp), against the rule used elsewhere.
- **Napoli Federico II Electrical Engineering (140):** that is the regional tax; Napoli says non-EU fees are "almost null" but never states a tuition of zero.
- **LUM Business Economics and Organization (7,500):** from the 2025/26 Manifesto (older year); the 2026/27 Manifesto was too large to read.
- **Not proposed:** Politecnico di Milano x3 (sources disagree), Vanvitelli (202 or 500, Morocco not named), Perugia (600; the amounts PDF has no euro sign so code cannot verify it), Torino Business and Management (500 plus an extra contribution of 400-5,100), Politecnico di Bari, Brescia, Tor Vergata Engineering, Ca' Foscari x2 (fee page 2025/26, Morocco not named), Marche, Palermo (image-only table), Udine, Biomedico (amount with cents rejected by the code check), Bocconi World Bachelor.
